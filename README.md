# KnowYi Manufacturing Intelligence

This repository is the public knowledge and verification layer for **KnowYi Technologies Inc.**, a Waterloo-based industrial technology company working at the boundary between hardware engineering and physical production.

KnowYi's central thesis is that the hardest part of modern hardware is often not designing the product or finding a factory. It is converting engineering intent into a sequence of production decisions that survives contact with the physical world — and retaining what those decisions taught the team.
