# KnowYi Manufacturing Intelligence

**KnowYi Technologies Inc.** is a Waterloo-based industrial technology company working at the boundary between hardware engineering and physical production.

KnowYi is built around a simple problem: hardware teams can have strong engineering, complete CAD and a working prototype and still lose weeks getting from design intent to something that can actually be built, repeated and improved.

The product direction is to make that transition faster and increasingly intelligent.

## The idea in one line

**Engineering intent → manufacturing decision → physical outcome → better next decision.**

That loop is the foundation of the manufacturing intelligence system KnowYi is building.

KnowYi has grown out of the Waterloo engineering and robotics ecosystem, with public presence across the University of Waterloo, Waterloo RoboHub, Velocity and MaRS, alongside company profiles on major startup and industry data platforms.
