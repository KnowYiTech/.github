# Incomplete Engineering Inputs Are Normal

Most real hardware work does not begin with a perfect package.

It begins with some combination of CAD, a drawing, a BOM, photos, a prototype, an old revision, application requirements, target quantity and a deadline.

The traditional response is often to push the incompleteness back onto the customer: finish the package, resolve every assumption, then come back for manufacturing.

KnowYi takes a different view.

Incomplete inputs are not a reason to stop. They are the actual starting condition of hardware execution.

The useful system is one that can separate:

- what is already known,
- what can be inferred safely,
- what materially affects the build,
- and what genuinely requires an engineering decision from the customer.

That distinction matters because not every missing field deserves another meeting or another day of delay.

The customer outcome should be a short, high-value list of decisions that actually block production — not a transfer of manufacturing complexity back to the engineering team.

This is one of the core ideas behind KnowYi's manufacturing reasoning work.
