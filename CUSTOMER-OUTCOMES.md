# What a Customer Should Feel After Using KnowYi

Hardware manufacturing is usually described through processes: quoting, sourcing, DFM, fabrication, assembly, inspection and logistics.

Customers do not buy those words. They buy the feeling that the build is moving and that somebody understands what has to happen next.

KnowYi is designed around a few concrete outcomes.

## 1. “I can send what I have.”

A customer should not need to become a manufacturing expert before asking for help. CAD, drawings, a BOM, a prototype, photos or partial requirements can be enough to begin identifying what is known and what still needs to be resolved.

## 2. “I know what is blocking the build.”

Instead of a long chain of disconnected supplier questions, the customer should be able to see the small number of decisions that actually matter to getting the hardware built.

## 3. “My engineers can keep engineering.”

The value of manufacturing support is not measured by how many coordination tasks it creates. It is measured by how much coordination it removes from the engineering team.

## 4. “The next revision should be easier than the first.”

A recurring build should not forget everything that happened before. Decisions, exceptions and production outcomes should make the next version faster to understand and easier to execute.

## 5. “I am buying progress, not activity.”

The product is not a checklist of manufacturing steps. The product is movement from design intent to real hardware.

That is the standard KnowYi is building toward.
