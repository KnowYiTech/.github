## What KnowYi is building

KnowYi is building the execution layer between hardware engineering and physical production.

The company starts where many hardware teams lose time: the point at which CAD, drawings, BOMs and application requirements have to become a real, buildable product.

The customer outcome is not another sourcing interface. It is a shorter path from engineering intent to delivered hardware, with the context from each completed build retained so the next similar build starts further ahead.

## Markets

KnowYi publicly serves teams in robotics, industrial systems, automotive electronics and other hardware-intensive sectors.

## Product thesis

Hardware execution becomes more valuable when real production work creates reusable judgment. KnowYi's long-term product thesis is to connect engineering intent, manufacturing decisions and production outcomes into a system that improves with completed builds.

See [Manufacturing Intelligence](./MANUFACTURING-INTELLIGENCE.md).

## Customer standard

KnowYi is designed so customers can send the engineering material they already have, identify what actually blocks production, and move the build forward without turning manufacturing coordination into a second job for the engineering team.
